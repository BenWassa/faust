## Outstanding evidence and editorial gates

1. **National Bank:** need a permanent official/archival 2025Q3 report version before restoring Toronto burden; live link is overwritten.
2. **Demographia:** the exact unpublished 2024 Q3 median income and median-home-price *pairs* for each market require separate access to the report preparer's source tables. Do not infer a price from a rounded ratio; no monetary inputs are claimed here.
3. **London:** original Assembly report verifies subgroup/77% but its overview does not provide a specific survey observation quarter. Label **2024 report** rather than pretending the data were measured in 2024.
4. **Causality:** housing ratios don't establish that housing pressures **cause** workism, platform use or clinical depression. The thesis, analogies and five archetypes remain **interpretation**.
5. **Editorial approval:** nonvalidated diagnostic result nomenclature and strongly asserted mechanisms (e.g., “defense mechanisms”, workism as inevitable) should be reconciled with #3; no thesis rewrite in this PR.
6. **Evidence refresh:** no automatic switch to 2026 data or cadence established. Decision **#4** remains owner-owned.
7. **Format/CI:** see PR validation report. Source URLs manually resolved during research; future availability cannot be guaranteed.
